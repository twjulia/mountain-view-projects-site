What’s changing

The city is planning an outdoor pickleball facility as the first phase of a future park at the Clyde Avenue site.

  • Preliminary concepts are being developed for 485 and 495 Clyde Avenue.
  • The October 12 community meeting has passed. The city published an online feedback window through October 21, 2026 for the preliminary concepts.
  • The city describes the pickleball facility as a first phase, with a future city park planned for a later phase.
  • The city lists December 15, 2026 for Council consideration of a preferred concept. This is an anticipated next step, not a confirmed agenda item.
