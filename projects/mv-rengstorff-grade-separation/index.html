A new way across the Rengstorff tracks

Rengstorff Avenue & Central Expressway

Rengstorff Avenue Grade Separation · CIP 22-27

Design & right-of-way
What you can do now

What’s changing

Plans would lower the intersection beneath the Caltrain tracks and add new walking, biking, and street connections.

  • The planned road crossing would pass beneath the tracks, with trains remaining near their current elevation.
  • Plans include a bicycle and pedestrian overcrossing, a Leland Avenue connector, and a Crisanto Avenue cul-de-sac.
  • The current city listing reports that 65% design was submitted in May 2026. It also reports a $99 million funding gap.
  • Spring 2029 is the city’s anticipated completion for Phase I, the Leland Avenue realignment. It is not a completion date for the entire crossing.
