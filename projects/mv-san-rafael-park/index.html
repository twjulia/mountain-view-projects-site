What’s changing

The city is preparing a 2.40-acre park off San Rafael Avenue, with design consultant selection and site clearing still ahead.

  • The city expects demolition at 939 San Rafael Avenue in early 2027 and an initial community meeting in summer 2027. Neither has a confirmed date here.
  • Additional land at 975 Terra Bella Avenue is planned for a second phase whose design and construction funding has not been allocated. Summer 2030 is the city’s completion estimate.
